## Catalogue finding

The hosted Carrickmines preview returned Velocity MLT first for adult Balanced at club and advanced levels. Both Babolat Syn Gut variants were available as hybrid crosses under the nylon category. Balanced requires the synthetic gut category and therefore returned the full-bed fallback.

## Confirmed in follow-up

- Choose polyester by shape and the player’s scenario. Co-Focus and Focus Hex illustrate round and shaped constructions.
- With unknown tension, use 48 lb for the competitive 15–17 Spin case when supported. Respect a known tension or an explicit request for stringer help.
- For an intermediate player who declines polyester, prefer multifilament over synthetic gut.

No validation cases have been run. Resume with the unresolved case of a club doubles player who likes their multifilament but breaks it sooner than they would like.
