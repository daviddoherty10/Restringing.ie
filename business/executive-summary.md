# Restringing.ie

## Executive summary

24 November 2026 | Pre-launch business proposal

Restringing.ie is developing an Irish marketplace for tennis racket restringing. Players would find a local stringer, choose a setup and track their racket through to collection. Stringing businesses would manage bookings, stock and service records in the same application.

The founder’s existing restringing customers are the first group to invite onto the site. Offer online string selection, booking and locker drop-off and collection at one indoor host. Five to ten invited customers will test the complete process before public launch.

### Product position

The repository contains discovery, full-bed and hybrid booking, tension selection, guided recommendations, tracking, conversations and stringer-management workflows. It describes a product under development, with a public coming-soon page and a separate preview. Customers currently pay cash directly to the stringer. Stripe subscription code exists for business accounts, subject to configuration and validation. Online customer payments and tournament-specific software remain future work. [Repository evidence](https://github.com/daviddoherty10/Restringing.ie/blob/5ec1eb10d4f60601bbb39c52a9fc17f7edb8078d/README.md)

### Market and offer

Published Irish examples start at €24 to €30 including string; own-string labour examples range from €15 to €20. Service quality and convenient handover already matter to players, so Restringing.ie must make an existing process easier and bring useful repeat demand to participating businesses. [Dublin](https://racketservicedublin.com/), [Arklow](https://www.bighittennisacademy.com/restringing), [Tennis.ie](https://tennis.ie/pages/racket-restringing-service)

The central market illustration is 84,000 Republic players buying two €30 restrings annually, or €5.04 million in service spending. Player count and buying frequency are assumptions. The player count rounds an assumed 90% Republic share of Tennis Ireland's 93,807 all-island memberships in 2024; it is not a verified customer population. Platform revenue would be a small share of this service economy. [Tennis Ireland](https://www.tennisireland.ie/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Tennis-Ireland-2024-Annual-Report-Final.pdf)

The first pricing test is €15 monthly for businesses exceeding a five-booking allowance. The implemented rule counts accepted bookings per business per Irish calendar month, while the original proposal referred to completed jobs. Agree and disclose the final rule before taking subscriptions.

### Financial case

| Base net platform revenue | €1,080 | €12,762 | €29,521 |

| Optimistic net platform revenue | €2,359 | €40,478 | €137,771 |
| Optimistic operating surplus / (deficit) | (€9,594) | (€26,071) | €2,593 |

These are planning scenarios, not actual sales. Operating results are before tax, interest and depreciation. Prices include a modelled 23% VAT reserve, and costs include budgeted founder/contractor cash costs from year two. The base case reaches only about 68 monthly paid marketplace equivalents in 2031 after free-tier and seasonal adjustments.

The €15 subscription alone does not support a full-time business on these assumptions. The optimistic case depends on 500 separate pro-tool accounts on an annual-average basis and 80 tournament licences by 2031, requiring validated international sales. It still records a €3,407 cash deficit in 2031 after new locker capex.

The software base requires €18,039 including its €3,000 cash buffer. A four-bay founder-built locker adds €3,841 setup and €810 for nine months of running costs, taking base funding to €22,690. The proposed €24,000 ceiling allows for slower receipts. Founder salary is excluded.

### Pilot and partner request

Build one test door before commissioning the four-bay cabinet. Confirm host permission, written custody insurance and electrical installation requirements before deployment.

At 4 to 6 founder hours a week, target a private trial in February and public launch in late February to mid-March 2027. Open public bookings after real handovers and a staffed recovery drill pass, alongside legal and production-readiness checks.

The locker pilot carries no separate fee. Track first completed online bookings, repeat use, support time and occupied bay-days to decide whether the channel is worth expanding.

The immediate request to potential partners and enterprise advisers is participation in a costed Irish pilot. Larger funding and international expansion depend on paid validation, appropriate legal review and a revised model that can fund the people doing the work.
