# Research sources

Prepared 17 November 2026. Links were accessed or surfaced in live web research on that date. Published prices are snapshots, not quotations. Supplier statements describe what the supplier advertises; no product demonstrations or customer references were independently verified.

## Irish market

1. [Tennis Ireland annual report 2024](https://www.tennisireland.ie/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Tennis-Ireland-2024-Annual-Report-Final.pdf). Indexed report text: 93,807 memberships, 190 affiliated clubs, 39,336 tournament entries and 231 club tournament permits. These are all-island figures and separate measures. The PDF exceeds the research browser's retrieval limit; relevant indexed extracts were available. Memberships and entries are not unique paying customers.
2. [Tennis Ireland: about](https://www.tennisireland.ie/about). Current page describes 192 affiliated clubs, almost 100,000 players and an all-Ireland remit. The rounded wording is unsuitable for a precise Republic-only denominator.
3. [Tennis Ireland annual report 2023](https://www.tennisireland.ie/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Tennis-Ireland-2023_Annual-Report-Final.pdf). Indexed provincial active-membership table: Leinster 42,094, Munster 14,115, Connacht 2,734, Ulster 5,657. This older database series differs from affiliated membership totals; do not add or directly trend the series.
4. [Tennis Ireland governance and policies](https://www.tennisireland.ie/about/governance). Lists the 2025 annual report. Its [PDF](https://www.tennisireland.ie/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Tennis-Ireland-Annual-Report-Final-1.pdf) exceeded the research browser's retrieval limit, and its numerical contents could not be verified. Obtain it before a formal funding submission.
5. [Sport Ireland: Irish Sports Monitor 2025](https://www.sportireland.ie/research/national-adult/ism-2025). Approximately 2.12 million adult sports participants and 48% participation. This covers sport generally and is not a tennis market estimate.
6. [Tennis Ireland: 2027 tournament application window](https://www.tennisireland.ie/news/2027-tournament-application-window-now-open). Evidence of the next domestic tournament planning cycle; no stringing-software procurement commitment is implied.

## Irish restringing services and prices

7. [Tennis.ie: racket restringing service](https://tennis.ie/pages/racket-restringing-service). From €25 including string; €20 labour only; most rackets within 24 hours. Its frequency guidance is advice, not measured customer behaviour.
8. [Racket Service Dublin](https://racketservicedublin.com/). From €24 including string and overgrip; €17 own-string labour; published 24 to 48-hour turnaround and cash/Revolut collection payment.
9. [Big Hit Tennis Academy, Arklow](https://www.bighittennisacademy.com/restringing). €25 budget or €30 premium including string; €15 own-string labour; standard two to four days.
10. [Galway Lawn Tennis Club pro shop](https://gltc.ie/pro-shop/). €18 where the player supplies string. This is a labour-only comparison.
11. [Ellis Tennis Dublin](https://www.ellistennisdublin.com/racket-restringing). Advertises independent stringer arrangements at €20 to €30 with an overgrip and turnaround of 24 hours to a week. Shows that a coach-led referral network is an existing substitute.
12. [The Racquet Here, Midleton](https://theracquethere.ie/Racquet-Stringing-p268312442). Evidence of supply outside Dublin. No price has been used in the model.

## Software, hardware and technical references

13. [Stringing Cloud product](https://www.stringingcloud.com/). Retail and tournament stringing software. Tournament use is the vendor's claim.
14. [Stringing Cloud pricing](https://www.stringingcloud.com/pricing/index.html). Packs include €20/100 services, €80/500 and €330/5,000. Italian page states VAT included. Exact arithmetic gives €0.20, €0.16 and €0.066 per service, before any different tournament unit consumption. Vendor headline rounded rates should not replace division of package price by units.
15. [Vending Sport products](https://vendingsport.com/products/). VendBox description includes racket deposit, stringing instructions and collection messages; 20-compartment tennis configuration. Public purchase price and an open integration API were not verified.
16. [Ricoh Europe: Care Sport case study](https://www.ricoh-europe.com/media/customer%20story_care%20sport_tcm100-80506.pdf). Locker-based racket rental precedent. It does not establish Irish servicing demand or installation costs.
17. [Wilson stringing instruction library](https://www.ecom.wilson.com/explore/help/product/stringing-instructions) and [Ultra Pro V4 16x19 instructions](https://storage.googleapis.com/wilson-public/stringing/UltraPro%2816x19%29_2022.pdf). The example specifies 10.7 m for one piece or 5.8 m mains plus 4.9 m crosses. One model is an example, not a universal cutting rule.

## Irish operating and funding requirements

18. [Data Protection Commission guidance for SMEs](https://www.dataprotection.ie/en/dpc-guidance/guidance-for-smes) and [retention guidance](https://www.dataprotection.ie/en/faqs/responsibilities-data-controllers/how-long-should-personal-data-be-held-meet-obligations-imposed-gdpr). Data mapping, lawful bases, retention, security and rights handling.
19. [CCPC: selling services](https://www.ccpc.ie/information-for-businesses/selling-goods-and-services/selling-services). Online service information, marketplace disclosures and cancellation/early-performance requirements.
20. [Revenue: VAT thresholds](https://www.revenue.ie/en/vat/vat-registration/who-should-register-for-vat/persons-required-register-goods-services-abroad.aspx). €42,500 services threshold, subject to other registration triggers. The workbook's 23% reserve is a conservative planning assumption, not a determination of the applicable rate or place of supply.
21. [Revenue: DAC7/MRDP](https://www.revenue.ie/en/companies-and-charities/international-tax/aeoi/dac7/index.aspx) and [registration/filing FAQs](https://www.revenue.ie/en/companies-and-charities/documents/aeoi/dac7-reg-faqs.pdf). Relevant activities include facilitated personal services; filing obligations require professional scope assessment.
22. [CRO registration](https://cro.ie/registration/) and [Revenue business registration](https://www.revenue.ie/en/starting-a-business/starting-a-business/registering.aspx). A business name and a company are different registrations.
23. [Local Enterprise Office feasibility grant](https://www.localenterprise.ie/discover-business-supports/funding-and-grants/financial-supports/feasibility-grant/) and [DLR eligibility](https://www.localenterprise.ie/dlr/dlr/financial-supports/eligibility-criteria/eligibility-criteria.html). Internationally traded services and export potential matter. Local personal-service or retail activity can fall outside grant scope.
24. [National Enterprise Hub: Enterprise Ireland Pre-Seed Start Fund](https://www.neh.gov.ie/business-supports/pre-seed-start-fund). Up to €100,000, in €50,000 or €100,000 convertible-loan-note investment, with validation and international growth requirements. Availability and eligibility need confirmation.
25. [CCPC: Digital Services Act](https://www.ccpc.ie/enforcement-and-regulation/digital/the-digital-services-act), [platform-to-business regulation](https://www.ccpc.ie/enforcement-and-regulation/digital/platform-to-business-regulation) and [accessibility guidance for microenterprises](https://www.ccpc.ie/enforcement-and-regulation/market-surveillance/accessibility/accessibility-for-businesses/european-accessibility-act-guidelines-for-microenterprises). Assess scope and exemptions individually; the service-provider exemption does not settle hardware obligations.

## Expansion comparisons

26. [LTA 2025 annual-report announcement](https://prod.lta.org.uk/news/lta-annual-report-and-accounts-show-growth-of-tennis-and-padel-in-2025/). British adult annual tennis participation reached 5.8 million in summer 2025. Annual participation is broader than frequent restringing demand and Britain differs from the UK.
27. [KNLTB 2025 figures, published June 2026](https://www.knltb.nl/nieuws/2026/06/knltb-groeit-hard-richting-een-miljoen-spelers/). 942,000 players across several racket sports, 679,129 memberships and 1,582 associations. These are not tennis-only service customers.
28. [FFT federal plan 2025 to 2028](https://www.fft.fr/actualites/plan-federal-2025-2028-la-feuille-de-route-fft). Federation passed 1.2 million licences across its disciplines. This is a scale indicator, not a comparable tennis-only denominator.
